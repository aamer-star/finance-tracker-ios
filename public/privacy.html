Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

Finance Tracker ("the app", "we", "us") is a personal investment tracking application. This policy explains what data we handle and how.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: If you create an account, we store your email address and an encrypted password to authenticate you.
  • Portfolio data: Transactions, holdings, watchlists, alerts, goals, and related notes that you enter or import. If you are signed in, this data is synced to our cloud so it is available across your devices.
  • No tracking: We do not collect advertising identifiers, sell your data, or use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the app's features: showing your portfolio, syncing across your devices, generating AI insights about your holdings, and fetching market data. Portfolio context sent to our AI provider is used only to generate your response and is not used to train models.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — authentication and encrypted data storage.
  • Market data providers (Yahoo Finance, Finnhub) — to fetch quotes, charts, and news. We send only stock symbols, never your personal data.
  • AI provider (Groq) — to generate portfolio insights. We send a summary of your holdings; we do not send your name or email.

Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete Account inside the app. This permanently removes your account and synced portfolio from our servers. Data stored only on your device is removed when you delete the app.

Security

Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS. Stored data is held on Supabase's managed infrastructure with access restricted to authenticated requests.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
